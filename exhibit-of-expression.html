The Concept

Two art forms.
One statement.

Exhibit of Expression pairs a poet with a visual artist. Together they create one collaborative piece where the artwork illustrates the poem. On competition night, the poet performs as the artwork is displayed. The crowd decides who wins.

The Format

How It Works

01

Enter Solo

Poets and artists each sign up individually through their own entry path. $10 entry fee per person.

02

Get Paired

We match each poet with a visual artist. You collaborate to create one piece together before the event.

03

Perform

On competition night, the poet performs as the finished artwork is displayed alongside them.

04

Crowd Votes

Ticket holders vote through the platform. Every ticket purchase unlocks a vote. Results are announced live.

The Prize

Prize Pot Split

The pot grows with every pair that enters. $20 per pair goes directly into the pot. The crowd picks the winners.

Example: 10 competing pairs = $200 pot. 1st takes $120, 2nd takes $60, 3rd takes $20.
