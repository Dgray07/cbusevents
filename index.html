How It Works

Internal connections.
External experiences.

Every Columbus event you attend was built by people connecting behind the scenes. CBUSEVENTS is where those connections happen, and where the results go public.

V
Venue
Opens the Space
Lists availability, capacity, and event types they support
O
Organizer
Builds the Event
Selects the venue, fills vendor tables, books creators and staff
C
Creators + Vendors
Claim Their Spot
Apply for vendor tables and gigs through the Collab Board
E
Event
Goes Live Publicly
Tickets and event page broadcast to all of Columbus
A
Attendees
Show Up
Discover, buy tickets, and experience what the ecosystem built

Every role on CBUSEVENTS has a specific job in the chain. Organizers are the connective tissue. Venues provide the space. Vendors and creators fill the experience. Attendees are the audience. The platform makes all of it findable, bookable, and payable in one place.
